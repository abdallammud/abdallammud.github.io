Hello!

I am Abdullahi Mohamud Ahmed, a researcher based in Mogadishu, Somalia, working on natural language processing for Somali and other low-resource languages, and a member of OgaalLabs. My work sits where computational linguistics, structured data, and open language resources meet.

I build reproducible datasets, models, and tools that give under-resourced languages the kind of computational footing that larger languages take for granted. My projects include the largest machine-readable Somali lexicon (46,314 entries, extracted from a print dictionary), a morphological analyzer that predicts part of speech and grammatical features from spelling, and data-driven studies of the language's structure.

My research favors clear, rule-based and statistical methods over black boxes, and it favors resources that are open, documented, and easy to reproduce. Every number in my work traces back to a script and a dataset, not an estimate.

I am currently extending this work into new projects and looking for graduate study and research opportunities in NLP and computational linguistics, especially in groups working on low-resource and multilingual language technology.
