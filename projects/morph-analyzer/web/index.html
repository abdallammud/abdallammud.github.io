Somali Morphological Analyzer

Type a Somali word. A suffix back-off model — trained on the 46k-entry Qaamuuska lexicon and validated in the Qaamuuska-NLP paper — guesses its part of speech, and for nouns/verbs, its gender or conjugation class.

Guesses only — this model has never seen most everyday text and is not a substitute for the dictionary itself. Built on aggregate suffix statistics only; no dictionary entries are included in this demo.
